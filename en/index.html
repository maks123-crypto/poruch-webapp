Three steps, then a story every night

Everything happens inside Telegram. No app for grandma to install.

STEP 1

Record two minutes of your voice

Read a short text the way you would read to your child. As you are: with breath, with a smile, no announcer voice.

STEP 2

Tell us about your child

Name, age, the pet name you use at home. What they love, what scares them, what is coming up: kindergarten, a doctor, a move.

STEP 3

A new story every evening

Within a minute the text and the audio arrive, in your voice. Your child hears you. You know you were there.

Questions parents ask most

Is it safe to hand over my voice?

The voice is cloned only with its owner's consent and used only for stories for your child. You can delete it any time with one button in the bot, and we do not keep it afterwards.

Will this replace a real dad?

No, and we do not want it to. The best story is the one told with a hug. Poruch is for the evenings when a hug is impossible: a voyage, a deployment, a border.

How much does it cost?

The first 500 families get it free. After that, a subscription cheaper than one children's book a month. For military families we are planning a free line together with partner foundations.

What do I need to start?

Telegram and two minutes to record your voice. No apps, sign-ups, or cards.

What ages are the stories for?

Ages 2 to 10. Length, words, and plot adapt to the age, and the story knows your child's name, what they love, and what they fear.

What languages?

Ukrainian and English. Russian is not offered.
